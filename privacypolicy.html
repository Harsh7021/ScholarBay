Privacy Policy

Welcome to ScholarBay. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with our community. This policy outlines how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use ScholarBay, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Data: Your name, email address, and academic details provided during registration.
  • Uploaded Content: Study notes, question papers, and resources you choose to share.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with the platform (e.g., downloads, views).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to provide and improve our educational services, specifically to:

  • Allow you to upload and download academic resources.
  • Personalize your experience based on your B.Sc. IT interests.
  • Maintain a secure and collaborative student community.

3. Resource Sharing & Ownership

By uploading resources to ScholarBay, you acknowledge that these materials will be accessible to other registered students. You remain the owner of your original work, but you grant ScholarBay a license to host and display this content for educational purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement modern security measures (including SSL encryption) to protect your data. However, as an IT student community, we encourage all users to avoid uploading sensitive personal information within their public study notes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at scholarbay@gmail.com.
